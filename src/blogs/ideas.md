## Idea Submission Rules

When adding a new topic:
1. Map it to an existing content pillar or propose a new one.
2. Identify the specific knowledge source (skills.yaml section, Industry_work.md chapter, phd_work.md program, research_ideas.md idea).
3. State the angle: case study, technical deep-dive, methodology piece, or industry commentary.
4. Assign priority based on search potential, customer impact, and content-market fit.
5. Verify the topic does not duplicate an existing queue entry.
